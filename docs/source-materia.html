MATERIA

The shapes of the MATERIA lamps are the quintessential simplicity and neatness. MATERIA design makes up of details and is subdued with slightly retro, geometric, and modernist abstract motifs. A melancholic inspiration, that is nevertheless craving to enrich the present.
The main cover is a pure european long-staple linen fabric by Dedar, embroidered and embellished with a coated cotton ribbon with a 3Dish tactile look.
MATERIA comes in three variants: single, double, and triple, to create a cluster of lamps with adjustable height (a pulley allows the wire to slide lengthening or shortening by 90 cm).
The style unravels a color-block harmony in terracotta, plaster, cement, and tar tones, combined with complementary colors such as ochre, and green.
All the fabrics are 100% pure linen, with the components in brushed steel and varnished wood.
MATERIA has the strength of a timeless lamp and the versatility of a suspension, which can adapt to any environment, to meet everyone’s needs.
